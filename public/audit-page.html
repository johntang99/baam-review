§ 05 · Frequently asked

The things everyone asks about the audit.

Is the Review Audit really free? What’s the catch?
No catch. The audit is genuinely free — no card asked, no hidden tier, no time-limited trial. We hope some businesses, after seeing what the audit recommends, will want help executing the action plan — that’s what the Review Service is for. The audit alone has no cost and no obligation.
Why do I need to sign up if it’s free?
So we can email your PDF and so you can come back to view past audits. We don’t sell email lists. Same account works for both the audit and the service — if you upgrade later, no re-signup needed.
How many audits can I run?
Two audits per month, five audits lifetime. Generous enough to audit a few businesses you own; restrictive enough to prevent abuse. Re-audit monthly to track your Review Score over time.
My business is mainly in Chinese — does that work?
是。For Chinese-name businesses, we auto-detect and produce both English and Traditional Chinese versions of the audit. 對於主要服務華語客戶的企業，我們會自動生成中英雙語版本。
How accurate is the Review Score?
The score is calculated from four sub-scores (rating, volume, 30-day velocity, 6-month velocity), each weighted against verified industry benchmarks. The full rubric and per-vertical reference tables are on Page 07 of the audit — the methodology is transparent. No fake reviews, no shortcuts, ever — we won’t inflate your numbers and we won’t recommend anything we wouldn’t do for our own clients.
How is this different from Birdeye or Podium?
Birdeye and Podium are review management platforms ($299+/mo with setup fees). We’re an audit (free) plus an optional follow-on Review Service ($99–$399/mo, no setup). The audit gives you the analysis before you commit to anything — including us.
What if my business has no reviews yet?
The audit needs at least 5 Google reviews to produce meaningful analysis. Below that, results are unreliable. If you have fewer than 5, we recommend collecting some first, then coming back — or talk to us about the Full Service tier, which includes a setup phase for businesses starting from zero.
