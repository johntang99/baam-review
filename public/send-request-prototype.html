Design changes vs. current
  • Input fields are pure white (#FFFFFF), with a stronger 1.5px border for definition against the cream page bg.
  • Labels are 700-weight ink instead of the previous 500-weight soft grey — easier to scan.
  • Tips moved below the send buttons (instead of between the form and the buttons). Less interruption to the form flow.
  • Variant A (stacked): minimal visual change from current, just the above polish. Compact for narrower viewports.
  • Variant B (left-label): classic form-row pattern. Easier scan when filling many fields, more space for input content. Best on wide screens.
